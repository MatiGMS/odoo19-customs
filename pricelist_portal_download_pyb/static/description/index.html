Product Pricelist Portal Download

Empower your customers to download personalized pricelists in PDF or Excel format directly from the portal. Perfect for B2B businesses with advanced filtering and stock visibility options.

Key Features

PDF & Excel Export

Download pricelists in professional PDF or Excel format with complete product details and pricing.

Multi-Category Selection

Select multiple product categories at once to filter and download specific product groups.

Stock Availability Filter

Filter products by stock status - show only in-stock, out-of-stock, or all products.

Group by Category

Organize pricelist products by category for better readability and organization.

Stock Display Control

Configure per customer to show actual stock quantity or just stock status (In Stock/Out of Stock).

Customer-Specific Access

Enable pricelist download for specific customers with personalized pricing and stock visibility.
